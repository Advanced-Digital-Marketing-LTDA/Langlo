## Matching rule

Typed answers ignore letter case, extra spaces, apostrophe style and final `.`, `!` or `?`. Accents and every other character count, so `a` for `à` and `etudie` for `étudie` are rejected. Multiple-choice answers must match exactly. The rule lives in the module and the test; the app has no server-side evaluator yet (LANGLO-41 to 44).
