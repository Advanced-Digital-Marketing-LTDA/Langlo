## Audit and review outcomes

- **French A1 is explicitly a selected subset.** It now includes bounded objectives for passé composé, passé récent, present-tense reflexives, `il y a`, `il faut` + infinitive, fixed polite request forms, nationality/profession gender forms, and `être en train de`. The FEI A1 inventory contains additional material; known omissions include broader question forms, extended negation, and other adverbial/pronominal patterns. This catalog does not claim complete FEI coverage. Adult DELF A1 tasks corroborate identity/nationality and simple service interactions but do not supply a grammar syllabus.
- **German A2 outcomes are framed as Langlo instructional judgment.** The detailed Fit in Deutsch 2 list is a youth exam topic reference. Adult Goethe A2 materials confirm four-skill everyday communicative tasks and offer an adult course sequence, but do not independently establish every grammar objective as productive A2 mastery. German A2 rows therefore use `local-alignment`, including `de-a2-020`: it was `provisional` pending a decision on the breadth of `zu` + infinitive, and on 2026-10-15 the owner approved it as `local-alignment`, limited to a set of frequent verb patterns.
- **Prerequisite edges mean recommended teaching order.** They guide lesson recommendations and must not lock learners out based on mastery. Import validation is still strict for missing/duplicate IDs, dangling/self/cross-language edges, invalid level direction, and cycles. Future mastery gates require separate thresholds and a separate contract.
- **Sequencing was tightened:** French possessives, demonstratives, and partitives follow gender/number foundations; `aller + infinitive` follows the explicit frequent-present `aller` objective; French and German imperatives follow present/clause foundations; German separable verbs follow verb-second/present forms; and German A2 Perfekt expands `de-a1-018`. German articles now distinguish gender/article recognition (`de-a1-007`) from productive choice (`de-a1-011`). `kein` recognition (`de-a1-006`) is distinct from productive `kein` (`de-a1-025`); `de-a1-013` is demonstratives only.
- **English boundaries are intentional local sequencing.** Cambridge A1 Movers includes superlatives and some modal/infinitive patterns, while British Council's adult-oriented resource groups topics over A1–A2. The inventory uses direct comparisons, `can`, `have got`, `there is/are`, and `want to` as bounded A1 objectives; it places `could`, superlatives, and a broader set of infinitive/gerund patterns later. This is an adult-course sequencing choice, not a CEFR rule or error.
- **Bundled items were split:** `en-a1-002` retains the original combined meaning as a retired row; active `en-a1-019` covers `have got` and `en-a1-024` covers `be`. `en-a2-007` covers `could`, while `en-a1-020` introduces `can`; `en-a2-010` covers selected to-infinitives and `en-a2-023` selected -ing patterns. Existing IDs were not reassigned to unrelated topics.
- **Stable meaning and learner history:** the earlier `en-a1-002` objective combined `be` and `have got`. It is retained as a retired catalog record and replaced by active `en-a1-019` and `en-a1-024`. Prerequisites point to the active `be` objective; the replacement skills do not inherit prior mastery. English A2 preposition + -ing patterns now sit with `en-a2-023`; `en-a2-010` is limited to selected to-infinitive complements.
- **Review findings triaged:** sentence breaks were added to all 23 German A2 placement rationales; row-width, required-field, evidence-status, supported-track, lifecycle, source, replacement and malformed-CSV checks were added with negative cases; test data loads from manifest paths; the content version is a pre-release; the French subset is named in the manifest; and CSV/JSON/registry hashes are checked. The English preposition + -ing objective and its prerequisite edge were corrected. These corrections are included in PR head `cd3b84f`; PR replies and thread resolution await owner approval before PR updates.
- French A2 is outside this task. The absence of an A2 French inventory is recorded as a scope gap rather than implied coverage.
- **Review limits and PR state:** the owner review of PR #8 was diff-only. The reviewer did not run checks or verify source accuracy or language correctness. PR #8 is open and not a draft; this evidence records the state without changing the PR.
